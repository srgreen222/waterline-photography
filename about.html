About the artist

How she sees the world

She sees the world as colour first. A row of ochre and oxblood houses in Nyhavn becomes a tapestry; a yellow hull in Christianshavn becomes a brushstroke across dark green water.

Her photographs slow that moment down: the few seconds when a city's colours, broken up by the current, turn into something closer to painting than to photography.

The current series was made along the canals of Copenhagen, where the old harbour facades are painted in the colours she keeps returning to.
