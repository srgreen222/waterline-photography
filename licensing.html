Licence terms in brief

What you can and can't do

You can

  • Use the image for as long as you like, within the uses of your licence
  • Crop, resize, adjust colour and add text
  • Use it worldwide

You can't

  • Resell, share or give away the image file
  • Use it in a logo or trademark
  • Use it to train or prompt AI models
  • Sell it as a print or on products without a custom licence
  • Suggest that the photographer endorses your brand

This is a summary. The full licence agreement is sent with your quote, and the copyright always stays with the photographer.

Questions

Licensing FAQ

Do I own the copyright after buying a licence?

No. You buy permission to use the photograph in the ways your licence describes. The copyright stays with the photographer, which is standard for photography licensing.

How long can I use the image?

For as long as you like, within the uses of your licence. Exclusive licences have an agreed exclusivity period of 1 year, 3 years or permanently.

Can I edit the photograph?

Yes. You can crop, resize, adjust the colour and add text or graphics. You can't present an edited version as your own artwork.

Do I have to credit the photographer?

Credit isn't required for Web & Social or Commercial use, but it is always appreciated: "Photo: Waterline Photography".

Can I buy a print for my wall?

Prints are sold separately from digital licences. Get in touch and we'll arrange a print in the size you need.
